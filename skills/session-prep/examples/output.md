# Session: The Beacon at Halcyon Ring

> **Status:** Draft
> **Writing style:** Clear and simple
> **Destination:** Drafts for GM review
> **GM review:** Required before play

## Grounding

### Session promise

The crew must decide whom to trust while a quiet station becomes more
dangerous with every delay.

### Sources used

- Halcyon Ring setting notes
- *Mothlight* crew and delivery notes

### Facts and proposals

#### Established by the references

- Halcyon Ring is a small refueling station in the Vesper Reach.
- Ilya Venn manages the station.
- The crew has not opened the sealed case.
- The case came from an unknown sender on Port Meridian.

#### Proposals for GM review

- The case contains a navigation core that can restore the traffic beacon.
- One claimant wants the core hidden rather than delivered.
- The beacon went dark because someone is avoiding a patrol route.

These details are proposals, not established campaign facts.

## Eight-step preparation

### 1. Review the characters

| Character | Goal or connection | Player interest |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot | Keep the *Mothlight* independent | Difficult choices |
| Mechanic | Connection to Port Meridian | Investigation and personal stakes |
| Crew | Negotiate before fighting | Negotiation and hard choices |

### 2. Create a strong start

- Where play begins: The *Mothlight* docks at Halcyon Ring.
- What is already happening: The traffic beacon is dark, workers are arguing,
  and three people ask for the sealed case.
- Why the crew is close to the action: Ilya Venn asks them to wait while the
  station is unstable.

### 3. Outline potential scenes

- The crew interviews the claimants and compares their stories.
- The crew investigates the beacon and finds evidence before choosing a side.
- The crew leaves with the case, forcing the station to respond without help.

### 4. Define secrets and clues

- The beacon was switched off from inside the station.
- The maintenance team left behind a tool marked with Port Meridian's seal.
- The case reacts when brought near the beacon controls.

### 5. Develop fantastic locations

#### Halcyon Ring docking spine

- Evocative detail: cold station lights flicker across the ship's hull.
- Pressure: ships are waiting for fuel while the beacon remains offline.
- Possible discovery: a maintenance panel shows signs of a deliberate bypass.

#### Beacon control room

- Evocative detail: a silent navigation display repeats the last valid route.
- Pressure: restoring power may reveal the station to a nearby patrol.
- Possible discovery: the case responds to the control system.

### 6. Outline important NPCs

| Person | Session connection | Motivation or memorable behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ilya Venn | Station manager asking for help | Keep the station open; avoids panic |
| Sera Quill | Claims the case belongs to her | Pushes for a quick handover |
| Tomas Rusk | Knows about the nearby patrol | Watches for a chance to hide the case |

All claims and motivations above are proposals for GM review.

### 7. Choose relevant opposition

- A station lockdown may trap the crew while the beacon remains offline.
- A claimant may turn the workers against the crew if negotiations stall.
- A nearby patrol may arrive before the crew understands who disabled the
  beacon.

### 8. Select meaningful rewards

- Restoring the beacon earns the station's trust and a safer route through the
  Vesper Reach.
- Giving the case to the wrong claimant creates a future obligation or threat.
- Keeping the case preserves the crew's freedom but leaves Halcyon Ring at
  risk.
