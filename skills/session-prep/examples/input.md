# Session-prep example input

## Session idea

The crew of the courier ship *Mothlight* arrives at the quiet moon Halcyon
Ring to deliver a sealed case. The station's traffic beacon has gone dark, and
three different people claim the case belongs to them.

## Reference material

- Halcyon Ring is a small refueling station in the Vesper Reach.
- The station is managed by Ilya Venn, who values keeping ships moving.
- The *Mothlight* crew has not opened the case.
- The case was entrusted to the crew by an unknown sender on Port Meridian.

## Party context

- The pilot wants to keep the *Mothlight* independent.
- The mechanic has a personal connection to Port Meridian.
- The group enjoys negotiation and hard choices more than tactical combat.

## Constraints

- Two to three hours.
- Focus on negotiation, investigation, and a difficult choice.
- Keep the setting system-agnostic.
- Use clear, simple prose.
- Save the result as a draft for GM review.
