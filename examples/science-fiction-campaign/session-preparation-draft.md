# Session Preparation

**Session idea:**

On Salazar 1, the characters will deal with Jungar, a crime lord selling Amnesia, an experimental
skill-enhancement drug. The drug is addictive, and long-term use can cause severe psychotic events.

## 1. Review the Player Characters

- **Mara Venn** — former Helix Meridian systems engineer who wants to expose the corporation’s
  machine-intelligence experiments.
- **Ivo Quill** — pilot and owner of the *Wayward Comet*, trying to keep the crew together and pay
  off the ship’s debt.
- **Sable-9** — exploration android with incomplete memories and possible signals from the machine
  intelligence they were built to find.

## 2. Create a Strong Start

Before the *Wayward Comet* departs for Ghanki IV, an urgent holo message interrupts the crew’s
preparations. Athena, Mara’s childhood friend, appears on the projection. She is crying and barely
able to speak.

> “Mara… everything feels so bleak lately. I hate my job, my life—everything.
> I feel worthless. I don’t know what I’m going to do. Please come to Salazar 1. I need you.”

The message ends.

The crew reaches Athena’s apartment above the financial district. Market data moves across the
windows, casting pale lines of light over an otherwise immaculate room. Several work terminals are
still active, displaying unfinished trades and unanswered alerts. Her belongings are neatly
arranged, but the apartment feels abandoned.

They find Athena in a quiet room at the back of the apartment. She is unresponsive, and the crew
has arrived too late.

## 3. Outline Potential Scenes

### The Silent Apartment

The crew investigates Athena’s apartment and the active trading terminals.

**Estimated duration:** 15–30 minutes

### The Trading Floor

The crew investigates Salazar 1’s high-pressure financial world and looks for people connected to
Amnesia.

**Estimated duration:** 30–45 minutes

### Jungar’s Operation

The evidence leads toward Jungar’s black-market network and may become a confrontation or combat
scene.

**Estimated duration:** 45–75 minutes

The estimated scene total is 90–150 minutes, leaving time for the strong start, transitions,
player discussion, unexpected choices, and additional combat during a three-hour session.

## 4. Define Secrets and Clues

1. Amnesia is sold as a skill-enhancement drug.
2. Repeated use creates dependence.
3. Long-term use can cause severe psychotic events.
4. Jungar is selling Amnesia.
5. Athena used Amnesia to remain competitive in high-volume trading on Salazar 1.
6. Mara’s boss is part of Jungar’s scheme and distributes Amnesia to employees.
7. Salazar 1’s institutions know about the practice but tolerate it behind a facade of individual
   freedom and well-being.
8. Jungar is a black-market dealer backed by WhiteLily, a major pharmaceutical corporation.
9. A WhiteLily agent was testing Amnesia and was responsible for Athena’s death.
10. Amnesia’s psychotic effect is intentional. A specific electromagnetic pulse can trigger users
    to become cold-blooded killers, and Jungar’s scheme depends on controlling a kill switch to
    blackmail Salazar 1’s society.

## 5. Develop Fantastic Locations

### Salazar 1

A financial world built vertically, where banks, trading houses, and data centers rise through
layers of controlled air and artificial daylight. Its plazas, transit systems, and public displays
present finance as clean, efficient, and liberating.

**Aspects:**

- Endless towers linked by transparent transit tubes.
- Market data flowing across glass and augmented-reality surfaces.
- Quiet wellness messages beneath relentless performance metrics.

### Athena’s Apartment

A high-level apartment suspended above the city’s financial glow. The rooms are designed to feel
calm, but the windows never become dark and the walls carry the distant pulse of markets that never
close.

**Aspects:**

- City light reflected in polished floors.
- Personal objects arranged with deliberate care.
- A constant undertone of ventilation, alerts, and distant machinery.

### Corporate Trading Office

By day, the office shines like a model of Salazar 1’s promised future: glass walls, artificial
sunlight, quiet music, and displays celebrating freedom, health, and personal success.
Employees move through the bright, transparent workspace under constant performance measurement.
The office looks humane because every pressure has been polished into a promise of self-improvement.

**Aspects:**

- Artificial daylight brighter than the real sky.
- Wellness slogans displayed beside performance rankings.
- Transparent desks and meeting rooms with no true privacy.
- Performance dashboards disguised as wellness tools.

### Hidden Commercial Level

Below Salazar 1’s shining financial towers lies a second economy. Service tunnels, abandoned cargo
platforms, and sealed commercial floors form a criminal underworld where contraband, forged
identities, stolen data, and experimental drugs move beyond official oversight. The city's
respectable institutions depend on this place while publicly pretending it does not exist. People
who could not—or would not—adapt to Salazar 1’s demands gather here, hidden from the polished
world above and treated as warnings about what happens when someone stops performing.

**Aspects:**

- Cargo manifests rewritten after midnight.
- Brokers who never use their real names.
- Security cameras pointed away from important doors.
- Corporate shipments mixed with illegal goods.
- Old machinery vibrating beneath the polished city above.
- People living outside the performance economy, kept out of sight.

### Jungar's Nightclub

Jungar's nightclub is a techno dance club buried in the lower levels, where the music is loud
enough to make conversation feel impossible and the floor vibrates beneath every step. Beneath
the pulsing lights and dense crowd, Jungar conducts business in a sound-suppressing bubble that
turns a small space into a private room without ever stopping the party around it.

**Aspects**

- Relentless techno bass felt through the floor and ribs.
- Strobing lights cutting the crowd into fragments.
- Heat, sweat, and chemical sweetness in the air.
- A transparent sound-suppressing bubble for private deals.

## 6. Outline Important NPCs

### Athena

**Appearance**  
Athena still wears the immaculate clothes expected on Salazar 1's trading floor, but they have come
loose and creased. Her eyes are swollen from crying, and the polished image of a successful trader
has begun to fall apart.

**Roleplaying**  
Athena's voice catches between sobs. She keeps trying to apologize for calling, loses track of
what she meant to say, and then asks Mara to come to Salazar 1 because she cannot face
what is happening alone.

**Motivation & Background**  
Athena is Mara's childhood friend and a high-volume trader on Salazar 1. She has been taking Amnesia
for a long time to remain competitive in the financial sector. She wants help, but the pressure of
her work and dependence on Amnesia have left her feeling worthless and desperate.

### Jungar

**Appearance**  
Jungar looks more like a senior broker than a crime lord: immaculate clothes, measured gestures,
and a face trained to reveal nothing before a deal is complete. Only the watchfulness in his eyes
suggests how much of Salazar 1's hidden economy passes through his hands.

**Roleplaying**  
Jungar turns every conversation into a negotiation. He is courteous when courtesy is useful,
speaking like a merchant discussing an especially valuable piece of equipment. In his nightclub,
he invites people into a sound-suppressing bubble before discussing anything sensitive, letting the
music continue outside while he controls the conversation inside.

**Motivation & Background**  
Jungar is a crime lord and black-market dealer selling Amnesia from an operation in Salazar 1's
lower levels. He is backed by WhiteLily. His scheme depends on controlling the drug's
electromagnetic kill switch so it can be used to blackmail Salazar 1's society.

### Igritte

**Appearance**  
A carefully presented corporate manager in a suit that seems designed by the same people who built
Salazar 1's wellness campaigns: immaculate, reassuring, and impossible to personalise.
Her smile remains in place even when an employee is visibly struggling.

**Roleplaying**  
Igritte speaks in the soft language of care and personal choice. Every recommendation sounds like
support, but each one steers employees toward Amnesia and back toward the performance targets that
are making them ill.

**Motivation & Background**  
Igritte has contacts with Jungar and distributes Amnesia to employees as part of the scheme. Her
specific motivation is not established.

## 7. Choose Relevant Challenges

### Follow the Distribution Trail

The crew must connect Athena's Amnesia supply to Jungar before Salazar 1's institutions erase the
trail. They can examine trading records, question employees, or follow physical shipments. A
challenging approach uses DC 15; a particularly risky or indirect approach may use DC 20. Success
reveals a route into Jungar's operation or evidence against one of his corporate partners. Failure
does not end the investigation, but Jungar or corporate security learns that the crew is looking.

### Jungar's Security

Three mercenaries protect Jungar's operation in the lower levels. Each carries an assault rifle,
frag grenades, and flash grenades. In the cramped industrial spaces, they use flash grenades to
separate intruders before closing off the exits. The crew may fight, evade, bargain with, or expose
them. Getting through preserves the chance to confront Jungar; a loud or prolonged response gives
him time to activate the kill switch or escape.

### Jungar

Jungar fights with a monofilament garrote capable of cutting through anything in its path. He also
carries a remote that can activate the Amnesia kill switch in people who have taken the drug. The
crew's immediate goals are to keep him from using the remote and decide what happens to the Amnesia
supply. Jungar may negotiate if he believes he can retain leverage. If he escapes with the remote,
everyone who has taken Amnesia remains at risk; if the crew secures it, they gain evidence of the
scheme and the means to prevent an activation.

## 8. Select Relevant Rewards

- Evidence connecting WhiteLily, Jungar, and the Amnesia trials, if the crew preserves the records.
- The kill-switch remote and control data, if the crew secures them before Jungar escapes or
  destroys them.
- Leverage with Salazar 1's institutions—or with people harmed by them—depending on what
  the crew reveals and to whom.

## Open Questions

- Who is the WhiteLily agent who tested Amnesia?
- Why does Igritte participate in the scheme?
