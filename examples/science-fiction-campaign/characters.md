# Player Characters

## Mara Venn

Former Helix Meridian systems engineer. Mara wants to expose the corporation's experiments with
machine intelligences, but she is afraid that the evidence will put her former colleagues at risk.

## Ivo Quill

Independent pilot and owner of the *Wayward Comet*. Ivo wants to keep the crew together and pay off
the ship's debt without becoming anyone's employee.

## Sable-9

An exploration android with incomplete memories. Sable-9 has recently begun receiving signals that
may come from the machine intelligence they were built to find.
