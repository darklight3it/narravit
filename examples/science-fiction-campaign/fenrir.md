# Fenrir

Fenrir is a dangerous cyborg bounty hunter who has been hired to capture the crew of the *Wayward
Comet*.

- He carries a smart-ammunition rifle.
- He is accompanied by three collaborators.
- He is secretly a clone of one of the player characters.
- The campaign has not established which character he was cloned from.
- His employer and the terms of his contract are not yet established.
