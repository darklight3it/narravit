# The Ghanki Reach

The Ghanki Reach is a frontier region where independent settlements, corporate enclaves, and
salvage crews compete for access to ancient transit technology.

The player characters operate the light freighter *Wayward Comet*. They take difficult jobs from
people who cannot rely on the major corporations, but their choices have made enemies in several
systems.

## Current situation

The crew recently recovered evidence that Helix Meridian is secretly buying pre-collapse machine
intelligences. They have not yet decided who can be trusted with this information.

The crew has accepted a contract on Ghanki IV. The next session begins when an urgent message from
Mara's friend Athena draws them first to Salazar 1, a different planet.

## Established facts

- Helix Meridian is a powerful corporate actor with private security forces.
- The crew has made enemies but is not currently being pursued by a known Helix Meridian ship.
- Salazar 1 and Ghanki IV are different planets.
- The campaign tone is tense science-fiction adventure with room for humor and unexpected choices.
