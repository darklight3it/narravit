# Narravit v0.1: Session Preparation

The first version of Narravit will prove one complete workflow: a GM gives Narravit access to an existing campaign folder, invokes session preparation, and receives an editable session document grounded in their notes and decisions.

This release implements the first part of the broader [Narravit experience]({{ '/experience/' | relative_url }}) while following the principles in the [Narravit design]({{ '/design/' | relative_url }}).

## 🚀 Scope

Narravit v0.1 should:

- Read campaign material stored as ordinary files.
- Find established facts relevant to the upcoming session.
- Report missing or contradictory information instead of silently inventing an answer.
- Let the GM choose whether to answer questions, request suggestions, or receive a complete draft.
- Distinguish established campaign facts, GM decisions, and AI suggestions.
- Produce an editable session-preparation document.
- Save accepted decisions to the campaign knowledge base only when the GM requests it.

## Outside v0.1

PDF styling, image generation, battle maps, and VTT plugins remain possible future capabilities, but they are outside the scope of v0.1.

## Open Design Question

The remaining question is how Narravit capabilities map to installable skills. The project still needs a precise definition of a Narravit skill and the files, instructions, templates, references, or scripts it may contain.
