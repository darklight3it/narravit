# Narravit v0.1: Session Preparation

The first version of Narravit will prove one complete workflow: a GM gives Narravit access to an existing campaign folder, invokes session preparation, and receives an editable session document grounded in their notes and decisions.

This release implements the first part of the broader Narravit experience described in the [design document]({{ '/design/' | relative_url }}).

## 🚀 Scope

Narravit v0.1 should:

- Read campaign material stored as ordinary files.
- Find established facts relevant to the upcoming session.
- Report missing or contradictory information instead of silently inventing an answer.
- Let the GM choose whether to answer questions, request suggestions, or receive a complete draft.
- Distinguish established campaign facts, GM decisions, and AI suggestions.
- Produce an editable session-preparation document.
- Save accepted decisions to the campaign knowledge base only when the GM requests it.

## Outside v0.1

PDF styling, image generation, battle maps, and VTT plugins remain possible future capabilities, but they are outside the scope of v0.1.

## What Is a Narravit Skill?

Narravit follows [Addy Osmani's definition of an agent skill](https://addyosmani.com/blog/agent-skills/). A skill is an on-demand Markdown workflow that an AI assistant loads when the situation calls for it. It tells the assistant what process to follow, what checkpoints to use, and what result must be reached.

A skill is a workflow rather than a reference essay. It should define when it applies, the steps the assistant follows, the questions or checkpoints it uses, the files it may read or produce, and its exit criterion. A skill may include optional reference material, templates, scripts, or other assets.

For v0.1, the session-preparation skill will help the assistant read campaign material, involve the GM at their preferred level, distinguish established facts from suggestions, and produce an editable session document.

By default, one user-facing capability is delivered as one skill. A skill may invoke supporting skills when a workflow requires them, but each skill should retain one clear purpose, input, output, and exit condition.

## Session-Preparation Skill Contract

The skill prepares a session using a chosen Markdown template. The template defines the preparation
method; the skill handles campaign context, the conversation with the GM, and the editable draft.

### Inputs and template selection

- The GM's session idea and campaign folder.
- A template chosen by the GM, otherwise the project's designated template.
- A rules reference when system-specific mechanics are needed.
- Additional inputs requested by the active template.

If no template is supplied, choose the bundled example that matches the request. An explicit
Session Zero request uses Narravit's
[Session Zero template](../skills/session-preparation/references/session-zero-template.md). Other
requests use the
[regular-session example](../skills/session-preparation/references/lazy-gm-template.md). Both adapt
material provided in Mike Shea's
[Lazy GM's Resource Document](https://slyflourish.com/lazy_gm_resource_document.html).

The Session Zero template is a flexible agenda the GM can prepare before the meeting and update
during it. It records confirmed group decisions, shared boundaries, practical agreements,
character connections, and open questions. It must not record private disclosures or attribute a
boundary to the participant who requested it.

The regular-session example adapts the eight steps from *Return of the Lazy Dungeon Master*.
Narravit adds its own descriptive defaults and expands adversary preparation into **Choose Relevant
Challenges**: brief, usable descriptions of combat situations, traps, and significant skill
challenges.
It is an adaptation, not a reproduction of Shea's template.

### Template responsibilities

The template defines its sections, their order, optional choices and priorities. Markdown comments
explain what to ask, what content belongs in each section, and how to present it. They may specify
counts, examples, descriptive style, and method-specific rules. The GM can override these defaults.

All rules specific to a bundled method belong in its template. This includes the regular-session
example's eight preparation points, ten suggested secrets, read-aloud opening, scene timing,
location aspects, and NPC fields, as well as the Session Zero template's agenda and recording
guidance. A custom template can use a different method and section structure without inheriting
those rules. Templates carry attribution for the methods they adapt. That attribution remains in
the template and is not copied into generated session documents.

### Skill responsibilities

Read the entire active template before starting. Gather its required inputs and work through its
selected sections one at a time, using their comments to guide both the interview and the output.

Distinguish campaign facts, GM decisions, and creative proposals. Report missing or contradictory
information. Explain confidence and supporting evidence in the conversation. Present creative
additions as suggestions for approval; when no proposal is supported, leave it empty and ask for
input. Let the GM accept, revise, skip, or revisit sections and choose their level of assistance.

Create a partial document at the start and update it after every accepted change. Preserve the
chosen template's structure, keeping unfinished sections visibly empty. The skill must not impose
the bundled template's preparation points on another template.

### Output and completion

Complete the editable Markdown document when every selected section is accepted or explicitly left
open by the GM. Include accepted session material. Keep method attribution in the template rather
than copying it into the session document.
Instructional comments, placeholders, interview history, confidence labels, and evidence stay out
of the output unless the GM requests a working or audit version.

Saving the session draft is separate from saving campaign facts. Update the campaign knowledge base
only when requested. PDF production, image generation, battle maps, and VTT plugins remain outside
v0.1.

The bundled workflow has a
[manual acceptance record](acceptance/session-preparation-v0.1.md) based on the example campaign.
